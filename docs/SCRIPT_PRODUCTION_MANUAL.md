# 📜 MASTER SCRIPT PRODUCTION MANUAL
## The 10-Million-View System for Stickman Cinema Scripts
### Mandatory Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for All Channel Scripts

> **The Core Directive**:  
> Writing a 10-million-view script for a stickman survival and science channel requires syncing narrative curiosity loops directly to your **3.0 to 5.0-second visual cut speed**. Because stickman animations lack photorealistic spectacle, your scripts must rely on **mechanical specificity, escalating stakes, deadpan visual irony, and relentless cause-and-effect pacing**.  
> Every writer and agent must strictly follow this manual prior to drafting any script.

---

## ⚙️ 1. THE CORE RETENTION ENGINE (UNIVERSAL SCRIPT LAWS)
Before writing any specific category, every script on the channel must obey four foundational mechanics:

### 📏 Rule 1: The 12-Word Shot-Sync Law
* **Pacing Ratio**: At a natural voiceover pace of 150 to 165 words per minute, a 3.5 to 4.5-second visual shot equals **10 to 14 spoken words** (roughly one punchy sentence or two short clauses).
* **Format Requirement**: Never write passive paragraph blocks. Write your script **one shot per line**.
* **Visual Density**: Every line must contain a **physical verb**, a **number**, or a **visual noun** that tells the animator exactly what changes on screen.

### ⛓️ Rule 2: The "Therefore / But" Chain (Zero "And Then" Writing)
* Never connect two paragraphs with "And then" or "Next."
* Connect every single beat using only three causal triggers:
  * **"THEREFORE..."**: Action produces a logical survival consequence.
  * **"BUT..."**: A hidden physics, biological, or mechanical flaw ruins the plan.
  * **"MEANWHILE..."**: An external threat escalates while the protagonist is busy.

### 🔄 Rule 3: The 90-Second Open-Loop Relay
* Viewer attention drops every 90 to 120 seconds unless a new micro-question is planted before the current question is answered.
* ❌ **Bad Transition (Loop Closed)**:  
  *"Now that our water filter is finished, let's build a perimeter fence."* (Viewer clicks off).
* ✅ **High-Retention Transition (Relay Open)**:  
  *"The charcoal filter gives you two gallons of clean water a day—but the boiling steam venting out your chimney just painted a massive thermal target on your roof for every raider within three miles."*

### 🎭 Rule 4: Deadpan Voiceover vs. Slapstick Stickman Irony
* **Zero Stand-up Jokes in VO**: Never write jokes into the narrator's voiceover. Keep the narrator **100% clinical, serious, and mathematically precise**.
* **Visual Slapstick Contrast**: The stickman visual direction acts out absurd, painful, or humiliating consequences. The contrast between a serious science narrator and a suffering stick figure creates viral replay value.

## ⚔️ 2. SCRIPT SYSTEM 1: EPISODIC SURVIVAL SERIES (8–10 MINS / EP)
* **Target Specs**: 1,350 to 1,550 Words | 126 Shots (14 Grids @ 9 panels) | ~4.0s Average Cut Speed.
* **Core Philosophy**: Episodic survival series live and die by **Resource Progression** and **The Escalating Constraint**. Viewers stay to watch a stickman upgrade from a helpless civilian with duct tape into an apex wasteland engineer.

### ⏱️ The Minute-by-Minute Episode Clock

| Timecode | Shot Range | Narrative Beat | Retention Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 – 0:35** | Shots 1 – 9 | **The Cold-Open Crisis** | Establish the lethal environment and immediate ticking clock in 35 seconds. |
| **0:35 – 2:00** | Shots 10 – 26 | **The Amateur Mistake** | Show what 99% of people would do—and why physics kills them immediately. |
| **2:00 – 4:30** | Shots 27 – 58 | **Blueprint Tier 1 (Scavenge)** | First functional build using household items; introduce the persistent HUD tracker. |
| **4:30 – 5:30** | Shots 59 – 72 | **The Midpoint Sabotage** | The Tier 1 build fails under unexpected stress or predator adaptation. |
| **5:30 – 8:00** | Shots 73 – 105 | **Blueprint Tier 2 (Mastery)** | High-IQ chemical or mechanical pivot with cross-section X-ray schematics. |
| **8:00 – 9:30** | Shots 106 – 120 | **The Siege / Stress Test** | Action climax testing the Tier 2 build against maximum environmental pressure. |
| **9:30 – 10:00** | Shots 121 – 126 | **The Cliffhanger Snap** | Surviving the climax triggers a 10x worse crisis; cut to black mid-tension. |

### 📋 Step-by-Step Writing Formula for Series Episodes
1. **Ban "Previously On" Recaps in Episodes 2 to 5**: Recaps kill first-30-second retention for browse traffic. Start Episode 3 directly inside the crisis left at the end of Episode 2, baking a 1-sentence context reminder into the action:  
   *("With the gas stations bone dry and 400 runners two miles behind your bumper, your diesel engine has twelve minutes of fuel left.")*
2. **Anchor the Screen with a Persistent HUD Metric**: Every series needs one signature numerical gauge ticking up/down:
   * *Car Series*: Fuel Remaining (Gallons) & Armor Integrity (%).
   * *Acid Rain Series*: External pH Level & Shelter Limestone Buffer.
   * *Sound-Hunter Series*: Real-Time Decibel Meter (Redline at 20 dB).
3. **The 3-Step "Blueprint Build" Cadence**: Whenever the stickman constructs a contraption:
   * **The Scavenge**: Name exact real-world items (*"Rip the 12-volt alternator out of a lawnmower and grab three copper refrigerator coils"*).
   * **The Assembly (X-Ray View)**: Explain the physics in two sentences using an animated blueprint overlay.
   * **The Micro-Flaw**: Add one realistic complication (*"The weld holds, but the added 400 pounds of rebar drops your top speed from 60 miles per hour to 38"*).
4. **The 15-Second Outro Rule**: Never wrap up an episode safely. At 9:40, resolve the episode's main problem; at 9:50, reveal the next episode's lethal crisis and cut straight to black.

### 📝 Series Script Formatting Template
```plaintext
[SHOT 01 | 0:00-0:04] VISUAL: Red speedometer needle slams to 0 MPH on a gridlocked 6-lane highway.
VO: When the infection hits, the interstate doesn't become an escape route—it becomes a ten-mile parking lot of fresh meat.

[SHOT 02 | 0:04-0:08] VISUAL: Stickman in a gigantic lifted monster truck tries to drive over a sedan, flips sideways, and gets swarmed.
VO: If you brought a lifted pickup truck, your high center of gravity rolls you over the first hood you climb.

[SHOT 03 | 0:08-0:12] VISUAL: Zoom into a beat-up 2012 turbo-diesel hatchback weaving between two semi-trailers with 2 inches of clearance.
VO: Your only shot at surviving Day One is a narrow-wheelbase turbo-diesel hatchback—and you have four hours to turn it into a tank.

[SHOT 04 | 0:12-0:16] VISUAL: Blueprint X-ray overlay highlights the glass windshield and rubber tires flashing red.
VO: Right now, your car has two fatal weak points: tempered side glass that shatters under a 40-pound elbow strike, and pneumatic tires.
```

## 🔬 3. SCRIPT SYSTEM 2: SOLO SUBCATEGORY A — SCIENCE, HISTORY & QUIRKY FACTS (15–20 MINS)
* **Target Specs**: 2,500 to 3,000 Words | 250 to 300 Shots | ~3.8s Cut Speed.
* **Core Philosophy**: Splits into two distinct narrative engines: **Historical Taboo/Gross-Out** (Medieval intimacy, surgeries, castles, toilet paper) and **Biological Deprivation Timelines** (30 days without food, 11 days without sleep). Both succeed by making the viewer physically feel the discomfort on screen.

### ⏱️ The Minute-by-Minute Standalone Clock

| Timecode | Shot Range | Narrative Beat | Retention Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 – 0:45** | Shots 1 – 12 | **Second-Person Empathy Shock** | Drop "You" into the historical/biological nightmare in sentence one. |
| **0:45 – 3:30** | Shots 13 – 55 | **Chapter 1: The Hollywood Lie** | Shatter the romantic movie version of the topic with gross/hard data. |
| **3:30 – 7:30** | Shots 56 – 115 | **Chapter 2: The Daily Nightmare** | Escalate the mundane biological misery; introduce the "Misery Meter." |
| **7:30 – 11:30** | Shots 116 – 175 | **Chapter 3: The Midpoint Absurdity** | Deliver the single weirdest, funniest, or most shocking fact at the 50% mark. |
| **11:30 – 15:00** | Shots 176 – 230 | **Chapter 4: The Extreme Peak** | Show what happened to royalty OR the body's organ-failure stage. |
| **15:00 – 17:30** | Shots 231 – 270 | **Chapter 5: The Turning Point** | The accidental scientific discovery or physiological survival limit. |

### 🏛️ Formula A1: Historical Taboo & Quirky History Scripts
1. **Use "You" Instead of "They"**: Never write *"Medieval peasants bathed twice a year."* Write *"It is July in the year 1340, you haven't touched soap since Christmas, and your wool underwear is currently hosting four hundred fleas."*
2. **The "Problem $\rightarrow$ Terrible Invention $\rightarrow$ Worse Side Effect" Triad**: Structure every chapter around historic human error:
   * *The Problem*: You have a raging toothache in 1650.
   * *The Terrible Invention*: The village blacksmith heats an iron rod to cauterize the nerve.
   * *The Worse Side Effect*: The iron slips, burning your tongue and locking your jaw shut for three weeks.
3. **Ad-Safe Vocabulary Swap (The Algorithm Shield)**: To get 10M views without ad-suitability strikes, let the stickman visuals show the action while the voiceover uses clinical or clever euphemisms:
   * *Anatomical terms* $\rightarrow$ "marital duties," "royal consummation," "the bedroom calendar," "ancient contraceptives."
   * *Gore terms* $\rightarrow$ "the 30-second bone saw," "cranial drilling," "barber-surgeon bloodletting."

### 🧬 Formula A2: Biological Deprivation Scripts (No Food / No Sleep)
1. **Dual-Screen Zoom (External Stickman vs. Internal Organ)**: Alternate every 3 to 4 shots between external symptoms (stumbling, hallucinations, shivering) and microscopic diagrams (liver enzymes, neurons firing, stomach acid eroding lining).
2. **Quantify the Body's Self-Cannibalization**: Deliver exact biological figures:  
   *("By Hour 72, your liver has burned through its last 100 grams of glucose, forcing your brain to demand 120 grams of ketone bodies per day by melting your visceral fat reserves.")*

### 📝 History/Science Script Formatting Template
```plaintext
[SHOT 01 | 0:00-0:04] VISUAL: Stickman wakes up tied to a wooden table in 1820 while three burly assistants hold down his shoulders.
VO: Imagine waking up on a wooden table in 1820 with a shattered femur, zero painkillers in your bloodstream, and a doctor holding a rusty carpenter's saw.

[SHOT 02 | 0:04-0:08] VISUAL: Doctor pulls out a pocket watch; a "PAIN METER" on the right side of the screen hits 100%.
VO: Before 1846, a great surgeon wasn't measured by how sterile his hands were—he was measured by how fast he could saw through bone before you died of shock.

[SHOT 03 | 0:08-0:12] VISUAL: Stickman bites down on a leather strap that immediately snaps in half; his eyes bulge out.
VO: If you were poor, your only anesthesia was a leather belt between your teeth and a shot of cheap gin that actually made you bleed faster.
```

## ⚖️ 4. SCRIPT SYSTEM 3: SOLO SUBCATEGORY B — TACTICAL TIER LISTS & DISASTER TIMELINES (15–20 MINS)
* **Target Specs**: 2,600 to 3,200 Words | 260 to 315 Shots | ~3.5s Cut Speed.
* **Core Philosophy**: Engineered for **Comment Section Warfare** and **Retention Spike Re-Watches**.

### 📊 Engine B1: The Scientific Tier List Formula (Vehicles, Bases, Weapons, Dogs, Jobs)
To prevent viewers skipping straight to S-Tier, use this **5-Step Anti-Skip Architecture**:

| Minute Mark | Tier Placement | Strategic Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 – 1:15** | **The Rules & The 5 Metrics** | Introduce 5 scientific grading bars so ranking feels objective, not arbitrary. |
| **1:15 – 4:00** | **The "Sacred Cow" Slaughter (F-Tier)** | Put the fan-favorite pop-culture pick (Tank, Katana, Costco) in F-Tier first to spark debate. |
| **4:00 – 9:30** | **The Specialist Traps (D & C-Tier)** | Contenders that excel at 1 metric (10/10 armor) but fail catastrophically at another (0/10 stamina). |
| **9:30 – 13:30** | **The Practical Workhorses (B & A-Tier)** | Strong contenders that survive 6 months before a subtle long-term flaw gets them killed. |
| **13:30 – 15:30** | **The "False S-Tier" Bait** | Present a contender that seems unbeatable—then reveal its hidden fatal flaw at the last second. |
| **15:30 – 18:00** | **The True Undisputed S-Tier** | An unexpected, low-tech, physics-backed winner that solves every single metric effortlessly. |

#### The 90-Second Simulation Loop (Per Contender)
Never just list pros and cons. Run every contender through a 3-Act Micro-Simulation:
1. **The Hype (15s)**: Show why pop culture loves it (*"The M1 Abrams tank weighs 68 tons and has Chobham composite armor that laughs at zombie teeth."*).
2. **The 5-Metric Scoreboard (15s)**: Flash the 5 metrics on screen (Fuel, Noise, Maintenance, Mobility, Armor/Utility).
3. **The Field Test Failure (60s)**: Put the stickman in the field on Day 14 and narrate the exact mechanical breakdown (*"You fire up the 1,500-horsepower turbine engine—burning ten gallons of jet fuel just to warm it up—while the 110-decibel exhaust acts as a dinner bell for every walker across three zip codes."*).

### 🌍 Engine B2: The Planetary Disaster Timeline Formula (Sun Disappears / Gravity Halved)
* **Logarithmic Clock Structure**:
  * **Phase 1 (Seconds 1–60)**: Immediate physical shockwave (friction loss, light loss, pressure drops).
  * **Phase 2 (Minutes 10–60)**: Infrastructure collapse (power grids, train pileups, highway carnage).
  * **Phase 3 (Hours 6–72)**: Biological panic & shelter engineering (insulating rooms, rationing air).
  * **Phase 4 (Days 7–30)**: Ecological death (frozen oceans, thinned atmosphere, crop extinction).
  * **Phase 5 (Year 1+)**: Deep subterranean / geothermal pockets where the last 0.01% of humanity survives.
* **The "Population Counter" Graphic**: Persistent corner HUD tracking `HUMAN POPULATION: 8,000,000,000` dropping at every phase transition alongside temperature/air pressure.

### 📝 Tier List Script Formatting Template
```plaintext
[SHOT 01 | 0:00-0:04] VISUAL: Stickman holding a gleaming samurai Katana slices one zombie, then the blade bends at a 45-degree angle inside the second zombie's collarbone.
VO: Hollywood has lied to you for forty years: if you grab a samurai katana during a zombie outbreak, you will be dead in less than four minutes.

[SHOT 02 | 0:04-0:08] VISUAL: 5-Metric Radar Chart pops up: Skull Penetration, Stamina Drain, Decibel Level, Durability, Utility.
VO: To find the actual deadliest melee weapon on Earth, we tested twelve iconic blades and blunt tools against five ruthless laws of physics and human anatomy.

[SHOT 03 | 0:08-0:12] VISUAL: Cross-section of a human skull showing 6.5mm thick parietal bone deflecting a curved blade.
VO: Because cracking a human skull requires 2,300 Newtons of concentrated force—and doing it fifty times in a row will tear your rotator cuff before lunch.
```

## 📋 5. PRE-PRODUCTION SCRIPT AUDIT CHECKLIST
Before locking any script for voiceover recording and grid storyboard generation, run it through this **6-Point Diagnostic**:

| Diagnostic Metric | Target Benchmark | How to Fix If It Fails |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. First 15 Seconds (4 Shots)** | Zero channel intro, zero throat-clearing | Cut the first paragraph and start directly on the physical disaster or counter-intuitive claim. |
| **2. Shot Line Length** | 10 to 14 words per `[SHOT]` line | Split any sentence longer than 16 words into two separate visual shots. |
| **3. Visual Verb Density** | At least 1 concrete action or diagram per shot | Replace abstract statements (*"It was very hard"*) with physical verbs (*"Your fingers freeze to the steel wrench"*). |
| **4. Number & Unit Frequency** | Minimum 1 specific number every 20 seconds | Add exact temperatures, decibels, calories, PSI, or hours to make the stakes tangible. |
| **5. Midpoint Re-Hook (50% Mark)** | Major twist or absurdity at 4:30 (Series) or 8:30 (Solo) | Insert a catastrophic equipment failure (Series) or the single most shocking historical/scientific fact (Solo). |
| **6. Ending Cut (Last 10 Seconds)** | Zero "Thanks for watching" or safe wrap-up | End on a cliffhanger snap (Series) or a punchy definitive verdict sentence (Solo) so viewers cannot click off early. |
